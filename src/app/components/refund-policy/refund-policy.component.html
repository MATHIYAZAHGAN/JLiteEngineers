Refund & Exchange Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

IMPORTANT: At JLITE Engineers, we take pride in the quality and precision of every electrical component we supply. This policy outlines our refund, exchange, and cancellation procedures.

Overview

At JLITE Engineers, we are committed to providing high-quality electrical products including MCBs, MCCBs, LED lighting, switchgear, and wiring accessories. We understand that sometimes products may not meet your expectations or requirements.

While we strive for 100% customer satisfaction, please note:

  • We offer exchanges for defective or damaged products
  • We offer refunds under specific conditions outlined below
  • All electrical components must be returned unused and in original packaging

Exchanges

We understand that product specifications and requirements may vary. While we do not typically accept returns for change-of-mind purchases, we do offer exchanges in the following situations:

Eligible for Exchange:

  • Defective Products: Manufacturing defects or malfunctions
  • Damaged During Shipping: Products damaged in transit
  • Wrong Product Delivered: If we sent incorrect specifications or models
  • Quality Issues: Products not meeting ISI/CE/ISO standards as advertised

Exchange Process:

To initiate an exchange inquiry:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  • Email: jlite@jliteengineers.com
  • WhatsApp/Call: +91 73581 78174
  • Provide order number, product details, and photos showing the defect/damage

Processing Timeline:

  • Exchange requests are reviewed within 24-48 hours
  • Approved exchanges will be processed within 3-5 business days
  • Replacement products will be delivered within 7-10 business days

Return Requirements

For an exchange to be processed, please ensure the following:

  • The product must be unused and undamaged
  • It must be returned in its original packaging
  • All price tags, original invoices, and labels must remain intact and attached
  • Seals on electrical components must be unbroken (for safety compliance)
  • All accessories, manuals, and warranty cards must be included

NOTE: Products with broken seals (except for inspection during delivery) cannot be exchanged due to electrical safety regulations.

Refunds

Refund Eligibility:

Refunds are provided in the following circumstances:

  • Order Cancelled by JLITE Engineers: Full refund if we cannot fulfill your order
  • Duplicate Payment: If you were charged twice by mistake
  • Product Not Delivered: If your order was not delivered within 30 days
  • Major Quality Issues: If the product fails to meet basic safety standards and exchange is not possible

Refund Process:

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

If approved:

  • Refunds will be processed to the original payment method
  • Processing time: 7-10 business days from approval
  • You will receive confirmation via email once processed
  • Bank processing may take additional 3-5 business days

Non-Refundable Items:

  • Custom-made or specially ordered products
  • Products with broken seals or tampered packaging
  • Products damaged due to misuse, improper installation, or external factors
  • Shipping and handling charges (unless the return is due to our error)

Cancellation Policy

Customer Cancellations:

  • Before Dispatch: Orders can be cancelled free of charge if not yet dispatched. Contact us immediately at +91 73581 78174
  • After Dispatch: Once dispatched, orders cannot be cancelled but may be eligible for return/exchange upon delivery
  • After Processing: Orders under processing (picking, packing) may incur a cancellation fee

JLITE Engineers Cancellation Rights:

JLITE Engineers reserves the right to cancel an order at its discretion for reasons including:

  • Product unavailability or stock issues
  • Pricing or technical errors on the website
  • Payment verification issues
  • Suspected fraudulent activity
  • Delivery to unserviceable location

In such cases: The entire amount will be refunded back to you within 7-10 business days to the original payment source.

Defective Products & Warranty Claims

All JLITE Engineers products come with manufacturer warranty:

  • MCBs & MCCBs: 5 years warranty
  • LED Products: 2-3 years warranty (varies by model)
  • Switchgear: 3 years warranty
  • Wiring Accessories: 2 years warranty

For warranty claims:

  • Contact us with purchase invoice and product details
  • Defective products will be repaired or replaced under warranty
  • Warranty does not cover damage due to misuse, improper installation, or external factors

Shipping Costs

  • Defective/Wrong Products: We bear all return and replacement shipping costs
  • Customer Error: Customer bears return shipping costs; we cover replacement shipping
  • Refunds: Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

Bulk Orders & B2B Customers

For contractors, electricians, and bulk orders:

  • Custom return/exchange policies may apply
  • Contact our B2B team for specific terms
  • Minimum quality guarantees for all bulk purchases

Important Notes

  • Always inspect products upon delivery
  • Report any damage or discrepancy within 24 hours
  • Take photos/videos during unboxing for evidence
  • Keep all packaging materials until product is tested
  • Electrical components must comply with safety regulations
