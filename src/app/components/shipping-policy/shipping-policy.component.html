Delivery Process

At the Time of Delivery:

  • You or an authorized person must be present to receive the delivery
  • Inspect the package before accepting - check for any visible damage
  • Verify the order contents match your invoice
  • Sign the delivery receipt only after inspection

If Package is Damaged:

  • Do not accept the delivery if packaging is severely damaged
  • Take photos/videos of the damaged package
  • Report immediately to our customer support: +91 73581 78174
  • We will arrange for replacement or return pickup

IMPORTANT: Any visible damage or discrepancies must be reported within 24 hours of delivery to be eligible for replacement or refund.

Unforeseen Delays

While we aim for timely delivery, shipments may occasionally experience delays due to:

  • Festivals or National Holidays: Increased shipping volumes during Diwali, Pongal, etc.
  • Extreme Weather Conditions: Floods, cyclones, heavy rains affecting logistics
  • Public Health Emergencies: Pandemics or similar situations causing service disruptions
  • Transport Strikes: Labor strikes or roadblocks
  • Remote Location Access: Difficult terrain or limited courier service
  • Customs/Interstate Checks: Documentation verification at state borders

We appreciate your patience during these times and assure you that we are always working to get your parcel to you as quickly as possible. You will be notified of any significant delays.

Failed Delivery Attempts

If the courier is unable to deliver your order:

  • 1st Attempt: SMS/Call notification sent; delivery reattempted next business day
  • 2nd Attempt: Final delivery attempt with customer coordination
  • 3rd Attempt Failed: Order returned to us; you will be contacted for redelivery or refund

Redelivery Charges: May apply for reattempts due to customer unavailability.
